# Privacy Policy for AbhayasGuru

**Last Updated: November 18, 2025**

## Introduction

AbhayasGuru ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application AbhayasGuru (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Anonymous Usage Analytics

We collect minimal, anonymous data to improve your experience:

#### First-Time Setup Data
When you first open the app, we may collect:
- Your exam preparation preference (e.g., SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway, etc.)
- Device type and operating system version
- App version
- Language preference

#### Usage Statistics
We collect anonymous analytics about:
- Number of times the Quiz feature is accessed
- Number of times Current Affairs section is opened
- Quiz completion rates (without identifying individual users)
- Time spent in different sections of the app
- Feature usage patterns
- Error logs and crash reports

### 2. Data We DO NOT Collect

We want to be transparent about what we DON'T collect:
- ❌ Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Contact information
- ❌ Photos or media files
- ❌ Device identifiers that can personally identify you
- ❌ IP addresses
- ❌ Browsing history outside the app
- ❌ Financial information
- ❌ Audio or video recordings

### 3. Automatically Collected Information

- Device information (type, model, operating system version)
- App performance data
- Anonymous crash reports
- App usage frequency

## How We Use Your Information

The anonymous data we collect is used solely to:
- Understand which features are most popular
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Identify and fix bugs and technical issues
- Make informed decisions about future features
- Generate aggregate usage reports
- Optimize content delivery
- Enhance quiz and current affairs sections

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- Your exam preference is stored locally on your device
- No personal data is stored on our servers
- You can reset your preferences at any time within the app

### Backend Storage
- All analytics data is stored anonymously and cannot be traced back to individual users
- Data is aggregated and stored on secure servers
- We implement industry-standard security measures

### Data Retention
- Anonymous usage statistics are retained for analytical purposes only
- You can reset your exam preference at any time within the app
- Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device

## Third-Party Services

Currently, AbhayasGuru does not integrate with any third-party analytics or advertising services. All data collection is handled internally by our secure systems.

**Note:** If we integrate third-party services in the future, we will:
- Update this Privacy Policy
- Notify you through an in-app notification
- Provide links to their privacy policies
- Ensure they comply with data protection standards

## Permissions

The App requires minimal permissions:

### Required Permissions
- **INTERNET**: To load current affairs content and app updates

### Permissions We DO NOT Request
- ❌ No Storage Access: We do not access your device storage
- ❌ No Camera/Microphone: We do not use camera or microphone
- ❌ No Location: We do not track your location
- ❌ No Contacts: We do not access your contacts
- ❌ No Phone: We do not access your phone or call logs

You can verify these permissions in your device settings at any time.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request information about the anonymous data we collect
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your usage data
- **Opt-out**: Use the app without providing any personal information
- **Portability**: Export your quiz preferences (future feature)
- **Transparency**: Know exactly what data is collected and how it's used

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. AbhayasGuru is designed for students preparing for government exams, typically ages 18 and above. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, please contact us immediately.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your information to third parties.

We may share anonymous, aggregated data only in the following circumstances:
- **Statistical Reports**: Anonymized usage statistics for app improvement
- **Legal Compliance**: When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (aggregated data only)

**Important:** We will NEVER share any personally identifiable information because we don't collect it.

## International Data Transfers

Since we only collect anonymous analytics data, there are no personal data transfers. Our servers may be located in different countries, but all data remains anonymous and aggregated.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Displaying an in-app notification for significant changes

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
